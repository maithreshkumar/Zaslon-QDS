## 1. System Model & Dual-Channel Topology

Zaslon-QDS models a decentralized, high-assurance digital signature network operating across three independent network actors:
- **Alice (Signer)**: Originator of signed directives, documents, and quantum evidence states.
- **Eve (Adversary)**: Active and passive man-in-the-middle adversary with interception and state mutation access.
- **Bob (Verifier)**: Designated recipient and independent cryptographic verifier.

## 2. Adversary Capabilities & Threat Assumptions

### 2.1 Adversary Powers (Eve)
1. **Classical Channel Tap & Eavesdropping**: Eve can intercept in-flight classical payloads, read plaintext documents, inspect the companion `.qds.json` manifest, and view public declaration tokens.
2. **Classical Payload Mutation**: Eve can substitute the document bytes (`payload.bin`), tamper with public declaration hashes in `.qds.json`, alter the committed SHA-512 digest, or modify envelope headers (e.g. sender ID, recipient ID).
3. **Quantum Channel Interception**: Eve can access the optical fiber link between Alice and Bob, inject Pauli bit-flip noise ($X$), phase-flip noise ($Z$), depolarizing noise, or alter measurement basis metadata ($Z \leftrightarrow X$).
4. **Quantum Intercept-and-Resend**: Eve can intercept the quantum qubits, perform non-orthogonal projective measurements in arbitrary bases, and transmit newly prepared quantum states to Bob.
5. **Replay & Timestamp Manipulation**: Eve can attempt to replay previously verified and consumed signature tokens, or backdate declaration timestamps to exceed freshness windows.
6. **Unauthorized System Probing**: Eve can attempt to probe Bob's private endpoints (e.g. sample indices, private verification evidence) or trigger verification without authorized recipient credentials.

### 2.2 System Invariants & Bounds (What Eve Cannot Do)
1. **No AI/ML Blind Spots**: Bob relies strictly on deterministic cryptographic checks (SHA-512 recomputation, token lookup, row-locked database consumption) and rigorous statistical hypothesis testing (Fisher's Exact Test). There are zero ML models that Eve can evade via adversarial perturbation or gradient evasion.
2. **Strict Protocol Blindness**: Eve never receives Bob's final verification verdict ahead of time. In-flight mutation actions report only transmission status (`MUTATION_APPLIED`, `FORWARDED`); Bob's evaluation happens strictly after delivery.
3. **No Quantum Cloning**: In accordance with the Wootters-Zurek No-Cloning Theorem, Eve cannot duplicate unknown quantum states without introducing detectable non-orthogonal disturbance ($E_{\text{obs}} \approx 25\text{--}50\%$).
4. **No Verifier Secret Leakage**: The deterministic 5% sample indices ($N \approx 205$) are generated by Bob using a private session-bound seed and are never stored in the `.qds.json` manifest or exposed over the classical channel.

## 3. Four Canonical Attack Families (13 Threat Vectors)

Zaslon-QDS categorizes threats into 4 physical/logical attack families containing exactly 13 canonical vectors:

### Family 1: Identity & Authorization Attacks
- **1. Impersonation (`IMPERSONATION`)**: Adversary alters declared signer identity in public declaration or envelope to claim Alice's authority.
- **2. Unauthorized Verification (`UNAUTHORIZED_VERIFICATION`)**: Authenticated non-recipient attempts to invoke Bob's verification endpoint.
- **3. Unauthorized Probe (`UNAUTHORIZED_PROBE`)**: Adversary attempts to query Bob-private resources (sample indices, verification evidence).

### Family 2: Artifact & Integrity Attacks
- **4. File Integrity Tampering (`FILE_INTEGRITY_TAMPERING`)**: Direct modification of document bytes on disk or in payload after SHA-512 commitment.
- **5. Declaration Tampering (`DECLARATION_TAMPERING`)**: Mutation of declaration commitment hash inside `<filename>.qds.json`.
- **6. QDS Package Tampering (`QDS_PACKAGE_TAMPERING`)**: Mutation of declared SHA-512 digest inside the package manifest.
- **7. Forgery (`FORGERY`)**: Synthesis of fraudulent signature tokens and arbitrary measurement states without authentic quantum correlation.

### Family 3: Protocol Attacks
- **8. Replay (`REPLAY`)**: Resubmission of an already-consumed single-use declaration token or expired timestamp ($> 300\text{s}$).
- **9. Intercept-and-Resend (`INTERCEPT_AND_RESEND`)**: Eavesdropping interception, measurement, and resending of reconstructed quantum states.
- **10. Session Substitution (`SESSION_SUBSTITUTION`)**: Cross-session grafting of cryptographic tokens from an unrelated QDS session.

### Family 4: Quantum Evidence Attacks
- **11. Measurement Manipulation (`MEASUREMENT_MANIPULATION`)**: Direct bit-flip inversion ($0 \leftrightarrow 1$) on Bob's observed measurement outcomes.
- **12. Basis Manipulation (`BASIS_MANIPULATION`)**: Forcing measurement in conjugate bases ($Z \leftrightarrow X$), inducing $\approx 50\%$ random error.
- **13. Quantum State Replacement (`QUANTUM_STATE_REPLACEMENT`)**: Phase perturbation ($Z$) and depolarizing channel noise disrupting Hadamard parity.

## 4. Multi-Layer Defense Barriers

Every received package is validated through 5 sequential defense barrier layers:

```text
Layer 1: Access & Authorization Barrier
  ├── RBAC Verifier Identity Guard (Caller == Session Recipient)
  └── Private Resource Guard (Sample indices restricted to Bob)
         │ [PASSED]
         ▼
Layer 2: Signer Identity Binding Barrier
  └── Declaration Signer Check (Declaration Signer == Session Creator)
         │ [PASSED]
         ▼
Layer 3: Anti-Replay & Freshness Barrier
  ├── Single-Use Token Check (MySQL: is_consumed == False with SELECT FOR UPDATE)
  └── Timestamp Freshness Window (Delta t <= 300s)
         │ [PASSED]
         ▼
Layer 4: Cryptographic File & Package Integrity Barrier
  ├── Streaming Exact-Byte SHA-512 Rehash == Manifest Digest
  └── Manifest Commitment Hash == Sealed Public Declaration
         │ [PASSED]
         ▼
Layer 5: Quantum Teleportation Fisher Exact Test Barrier
  ├── 4,096 CUDA-Q Deferred Measurement Outcomes Retrieval
  ├── Private 5% Sample Extraction (N = 205 operations)
  ├── Sample Mismatch Ratio Barrier (mu <= 3.00%)
  └── Hypergeometric Fisher Exact Test (p >= 0.01 vs 1.00% Baseline)
         │ [PASSED]
         ▼
FINAL VERDICT: VERIFIED (Cryptographic Receipt Issued)
```

If any barrier fails, verification halts immediately, yielding a deterministic, explainable security verdict (`REJECTED`, `UNAUTHORIZED`, or `INTEGRITY_FAILURE`), recording an immutable entry in the chained audit log, and generating real-time security alerts.
